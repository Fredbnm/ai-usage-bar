AI Usage Bar for Linux (portable build)

Run ./AIUsageBar. The meter shows one icon per AI in the tray and adds itself to the
app menu (~/.local/share/applications/ai-usage-bar.desktop).

GNOME shows tray icons with the "AppIndicator and KStatusNotifierItem Support"
extension (Ubuntu ships it). Without a tray, open AI Usage Bar from the app menu.

More: https://github.com/matheusvelloso/ai-usage-bar
